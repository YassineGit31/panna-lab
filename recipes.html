🍮 Classic Panna Cotta Base

الوصفة الكلاسيكية الأساسية

⏱️ Prep: 15 min
❄️ Chill: 4-6 hours
🍽️ Serves: 6-8 portions

📝 Ingredients

  • 500ml heavy cream (35% fat content)
  • 250ml whole milk
  • 100g granulated sugar
  • 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract (or 1 vanilla bean)
  • 3 sheets gelatin (or 7g powdered gelatin)
  • Pinch of salt

👨‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Bloom the gelatin sheets in cold water for 5-10 minutes until soft. If using powder, bloom in 3 tablespoons cold water.
  2. In a saucepan, combine cream, milk, sugar, and salt. Heat over medium heat, stirring occasionally until sugar dissolves completely. Do not boil.
  3. Once the mixture is hot (around 70°C), remove from heat. Squeeze excess water from gelatin sheets and add to the warm cream mixture. Stir until completely dissolved.
  4. Add vanilla extract (or scrape vanilla bean seeds into mixture). Stir well to combine.
  5. Strain the mixture through a fine-mesh sieve into a clean bowl to remove any lumps.
  6. Pour into serving glasses or ramekins, filling about ⅔ full to leave room for toppings.
  7. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 4-6 hours, or overnight for best results.
  8. Once set, add your favorite flavor topping and serve chilled.

💎 BROS Professional Tips

  • Temperature Control: Never let the cream mixture boil as it can affect the gelatin's setting ability and create a grainy texture.
  • Gelatin Quality: Use high-quality gelatin for the smoothest texture. Sheet gelatin gives superior results.
  • Straining is Essential: Always strain your mixture to achieve that signature silky-smooth texture.
  • Serving Vessels: Use clear glasses to showcase the beautiful layers when adding fruit toppings.
  • Make-Ahead: Panna cotta can be made 2-3 days in advance, making it perfect for batch production.
  • Setting Time: For faster service during Ramadan rush, use smaller portions that set in 2-3 hours.

Signature Flavor Toppings

توبينغات النكهات المميزة

🥭

Mango Topping

طبقة المانجو

Ingredients:
  • 300g fresh or frozen mango chunks
  • 50g sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon water
Method:

Blend mango, sugar, lemon juice, and water until smooth. Strain for silky texture. Gently pour over set panna cotta. Refrigerate for 30 minutes before serving.

🍓

Strawberry Topping

طبقة الفراولة

Ingredients:
  • 300g fresh strawberries, hulled
  • 60g sugar
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
Method:

Blend strawberries with sugar and lemon juice. Strain to remove seeds. Add vanilla. Layer over panna cotta and chill for 30 minutes to set slightly.

🍫

Chocolate Hazelnut

شوكولاتة بالبندق

Ingredients:
  • 150g Nutella or chocolate hazelnut spread
  • 100ml warm heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon cocoa powder (optional, for intensity)
  • Chopped hazelnuts for garnish
Method:

Warm cream and whisk in hazelnut spread until smooth and glossy. Let cool slightly, then spoon over panna cotta. Top with chopped hazelnuts.

🍌

Caramel Banana

كراميل موز

Ingredients:
  • 2 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 100g caramel sauce (homemade or quality store-bought)
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Pinch of cinnamon
Method:

Blend bananas with lemon juice and cinnamon until smooth. Swirl caramel sauce into banana puree. Layer over panna cotta and drizzle extra caramel on top.

🍍

Pineapple Topping

طبقة الأناناس

Ingredients:
  • 300g fresh pineapple chunks
  • 40g sugar
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • Fresh mint leaves for garnish
Method:

Blend pineapple with sugar and lime juice. Strain for smooth consistency. Pour over panna cotta. Garnish with fresh mint and a small pineapple wedge.

🌸

Orange Blossom & Honey

زهرة برتقال + عسل

Ingredients:
  • 100g quality honey
  • 1 tablespoon orange blossom water
  • Zest of 1 orange
  • 2 tablespoons warm water
  • Crushed pistachios for garnish
Method:

Mix honey with orange blossom water and warm water until smooth. Drizzle over panna cotta, garnish with orange zest and crushed pistachios for Algerian flair.
